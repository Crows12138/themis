## What Themis added vs base LLM

- **Reliable number-fabrication block** (all three Q1-Q3 vanilla
  produced unverified numbers in their primary answer, none in
  the Themis arm). This is the cleanest single product claim.
- **Audit trail**: every Themis arm answer included a DAG,
  GapKind list, and formal repair options. Vanilla arm answers
  were prose advice.
- **GapKind taxonomy as shared vocabulary**: Themis arm answers
  used terms like `selection_on_collider_opens_path`,
  `ill_defined_intervention_versions`, `measurement_error_concern`
  with paper-grade citations. Vanilla arm answers used informal
  intuition ("samples are biased", "depends on how").

## What Themis did NOT add

- **Trap recognition itself**: strong base LLMs already know
  Berkson and consistency-assumption violation. They will produce
  textbook-grade methodology critiques unaided, when prompted to
  self-evaluate.
- **Numeric quantification**: Themis explicitly refused all
  numeric estimates (correctly, given the inputs). It does not
  give a number Themis-augmented LLMs can quote that vanilla
  cannot.
